# Starve No More — Full Rules (Abridged)

This is the in-game reference. The full design intent is in `StarveNoMoreDesignConcept.md`. Day-to-day play is driven by the on-screen Phase Banner and the Help menu (?); this Notebook is your fallback.

## Setup

1. Click **Setup Game** on the Setup Pedestal.
2. Pick a path graph (Compact / Sprawl / Linear).
3. Each player picks a character. Read your one-time character briefing — that tells you what makes you different.
4. Day 1 begins. The Phase Banner takes over.

## Turn Structure

### Dawn
- Day Counter advances.
- Reveal top of the active Phase deck. Apply effects.
- Doom advances by phase rate (Phase 1: +1, Phase 2: +1, Phase 3: +2, Phase 4: +3 — adjusted by player count, see Help → Doom).

### Day
Each player has **3 actions**. Choose any:
- **Move (1)** — adjacent tile. Costs 1 Hunger. Rayman moves 2 spaces.
- **Gather (1)** — draw 1 resource at this tile.
- **Craft (1)** — buy an Item from the Market.
- **Cook (1)** — at a Crockpot, prepare a Recipe.
- **Fight (1)** — attack a Threat at this tile.
- **Rest (1)** — +1 Hunger or +2 Sanity. Own house: also +1 Health.
- **Cleanse (1)** — pay 1 Wood + 1 Cloth + 1 Battery + 1 Energy Drink → Doom -2.
- **Trade (free)** — same tile, both consent.

### Dusk
Declare which tile you'll sleep at. Public.

### Night
For each occupied tile, least-populated first:
- Draw threats per the location's threat rate (modified by Doom thresholds).
- Resolve combat or damage.
- Charlie check: no light source = 1 d8 Sanity damage + 1 d6 Health damage.
- Storytelling: Comfort Items used here for shared Sanity gain.
- Sleep:
  - Own house: +1 Sanity, +1 Hunger, +1 Health.
  - Someone else's house with company: +1 Sanity.
  - Alone in a non-house: nothing (often worse).

### Tick
- Every character loses 1 Hunger and 1 Sanity.
- Rayman loses 2 Hunger.
- Coco's allies lose 1 less Sanity here. Luca's allies gain +1 Sanity. Ellie's house gains +1 Sanity.
- Pass First Player.
- Check victory/defeat.

## Combat

- Attacker rolls dice = base attack + weapon + modifiers.
- Each 5 or 6 = 1 hit (1 damage to enemy).
- Each 1 = self-fumble (1 Health damage).
- Enemy then attacks back: roll its attack dice; each 5–6 = 1 damage.
- Group combat sums dice; return damage rotates highest-Health first.

## Death and Revival

- Health 0 = Down. Sanity 0 = Lost (also Down).
- Flip standee to ghost. Cannot act. May whisper one word per round.
- To revive: a Telltale Heart token (cooked at the Crockpot for 1 Cloth + 1 Battery + 1 Food + 2 Health from the cook).
- Revived character returns at half maximums.

## Doom

The Doom track runs 0–30. At 30, the team loses. Thresholds:
- 10: night threats +1
- 15: Market refresh slowed (1/day)
- 20: all -1 Sanity at Tick
- 25: bosses can appear in any phase
- 30: defeat

Push back Doom by Cleansing.

## Victory

Survive Day 7 with Doom < 30.

Bonus achievements:
- **Pristine** — all 5 alive.
- **Truth** — 3 Clue cards collected.
- **Hero** — defeat all 4 bosses.
