# Quick Start — Starve No More

## Goal
Survive 7 nights as a team. The Doom track must stay below 30. If everyone is Down at the same time, you lose.

## Each Day
1. **Dawn** — A Dawn card flips. Read it; do what it says. Doom advances.
2. **Day** — Each player takes **3 actions** in turn order: Move, Gather, Craft, Cook, Fight, Rest, or Cleanse.
3. **Dusk** — Each player chooses a tile to sleep at.
4. **Night** — Threats are drawn at each occupied tile. Fight or take damage. Sleep restores stats.
5. **Tick** — Everyone loses 1 Hunger and 1 Sanity. Day Counter advances.

## Three stats
- **❤ Health** — go to 0 = Down (ghost).
- **🍴 Hunger** — keeps you above zero or you starve.
- **🧠 Sanity** — go to 0 = Down (lost).

Below 3 in any stat: bad things happen. Read your player board.

## Trade
Players in the same tile may **trade freely on either's turn** — no action cost. Talk. Plan. The game wants you to.

## Light
Anywhere without a light source at night = a Charlie attack. Carry a Flashlight (Battery), a Lantern (Fire), or sleep next to a Fire.

## Tip
**Hover anything** for its rule. **Click "?"** at the top of the screen for the Help menu. **Click "What now?"** if you don't know what to do.

You don't need to read the rulebook. The game will prompt you.
